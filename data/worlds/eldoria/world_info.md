# Realm of Eldoria

Eldoria is a dark fantasy continent shrouded in the twilight of an ancient eclipse. Centuries ago, the Obsidian Spire cracked open, spilling shadow magic across the land and turning the northern reaches into the Howling Wastes.

## Key Factions & Locations:
- **The Obsidian Citadel**: Seat of the Grand Arcanists who harness volatile shadow-crystals.
- **Silverveil Haven**: A subterranean sanctuary beneath the whispering woods where outcasts and rogues gather.
- **The Howling Wastes**: Uncharted tundra populated by frost specters and corrupted leviathans.

## Magic System:
Magic requires balance. Casting incinerating spells or summoning ethereal shadow-blades strains the weaver's soul. Ancient runes inscribed on moon-steel armor provide protection against soul corruption.
